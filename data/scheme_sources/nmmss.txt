Scholarships to help selected students from economically weaker families continue secondary education.

Eligibility: Selected Class IX students must qualify through the State/UT examination. Covered schools are State Government, government-aided and local-body schools. Parental annual income must not exceed Rs. 3.50 lakh. Class VII marks for the selection test are at least 55%, with a five-percentage-point relaxation for SC/ST students.
