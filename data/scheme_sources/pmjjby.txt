Scheme: Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY)
State: National
Category: Insurance
Source URL: https://www.financialservices.gov.in/pmjjby
Reviewed: 2026-11-07
Evidence type: reviewed paraphrase of the linked official source, not a verbatim quotation.

Annually renewable term life insurance offered through participating bank and post-office accounts.

Eligibility: Individual account holders aged 18 to 50 may enrol through one participating bank or post-office account with auto-debit consent. Renewal can continue to age 55 subject to scheme terms. A 30-day lien applies to non-accidental deaths for new entrants and re-entrants.

Verified documents / enrolment records: Prescribed enrolment / consent-cum-declaration form; Auto-debit authorization

Evidence and limitations: The official FAQ describes Rs. 2 lakh cover on death due to any cause, subject to policy conditions, and an annual renewal premium of Rs. 436. First-time mid-year enrolment has pro-rata premium rules. This record is not proof that a user is insured. The document list covers verified enrolment forms only and is not exhaustive.
