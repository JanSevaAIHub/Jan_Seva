Voluntary contributory pension for workers in the unorganised sector.

Eligibility: Entry is for unorganised workers aged 18 to 40 with monthly income at most Rs. 15000. Applicants must not have EPF, ESIC or NPS coverage, pay income tax, or receive another government pension.

The source is a March 2025 ministry backgrounder. Contributions and enrolment must be confirmed by the provider; no active coverage is established.

Verified records: Aadhaar Card; Savings bank or Jan Dhan account details with IFSC; Mobile number
Official application portal linked by the ministry: https://maandhan.in/
