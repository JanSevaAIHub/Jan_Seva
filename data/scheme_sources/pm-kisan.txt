Income support for eligible landholding farmer families through direct bank transfers.

Eligibility: Landholding farmer families with cultivable land in their names are considered, subject to scheme exclusions. Higher-income exclusions include income-tax payers and specified government employees. The consulted national source is not limited to Punjab; it does not establish any particular person's eligibility.

Verified documents / enrolment records: Aadhaar information and e-KYC; Bank account details including IFSC; Land-ownership records; Self-declaration concerning exclusion criteria

Evidence and limitations: The reviewed PIB backgrounder describes annual support of Rs. 6,000 in three Rs. 2,000 instalments for eligible families. Land details, Aadhaar-seeded bank accounts and completed e-KYC are part of beneficiary verification. The listed records are verified enrolment information, not an exhaustive document checklist. No future instalment date was verified.
