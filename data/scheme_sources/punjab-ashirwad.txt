Punjab marriage assistance scheme for specified disadvantaged families.

Eligibility: The district government source states that the girl must be at least 18 and her parents or guardians must have Punjab domicile. It lists Scheduled Castes, Christian girls, Backward Classes, economically weaker families and specified widow/remarriage categories. Living in another state alone does not establish the required Punjab domicile.
