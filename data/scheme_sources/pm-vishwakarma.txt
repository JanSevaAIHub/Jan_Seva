Support for traditional artisans through recognition, training, toolkits, credit and marketing assistance.

Eligibility: Self-employed artisans aged at least 18 working with hands and tools in one of 18 notified family-based trades in the unorganised sector may be considered. Registration is limited to one family member; government employees and their family members are excluded. Similar government credit received in the previous five years can restrict eligibility, with specified fully repaid MUDRA/SVANidhi exceptions.

Verified documents / enrolment records: Complete document list not verified from this source.

Evidence and limitations: The September 2026 PIB backgrounder describes recognition, skill training, toolkit assistance, credit and market support. Applications are through Common Service Centres and undergo local, district and screening verification. This source does not supply a complete registration document checklist.
