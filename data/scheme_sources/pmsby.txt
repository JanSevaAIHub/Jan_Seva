Scheme: Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY)
State: National
Category: Insurance
Source URL: https://financialservices.gov.in/pmsby
Reviewed: 2026-11-07
Evidence type: reviewed paraphrase of the linked official source, not a verbatim quotation.

Renewable annual personal accident insurance covering specified accidental death or disability.

Eligibility: Individual account holders aged 18 to 70 at participating banks or post offices may join through one account. Enrolment requires consent to premium auto-debit. Scheme terms determine covered losses; account ownership and age alone do not confirm that insurance is active.

Verified documents / enrolment records: Prescribed enrolment / consent-cum-declaration form; Auto-debit authorization

Evidence and limitations: The Department of Financial Services FAQ lists Rs. 2 lakh for accidental death or specified total permanent disability and Rs. 1 lakh for specified partial permanent disability. It lists an annual premium of Rs. 20, subject to revision. Banks retain enrolment and consent records. The reviewed document list is partial; no blanket Aadhaar requirement is inferred.
