Pension support for widows in below-poverty-line households.

Eligibility: The Ladakh government lists widows aged at least 40 who belong to a below-poverty-line household.

Coverage is limited to the Ladakh implementation reviewed here; other states and their document requirements need separate review.

This is a partial reviewed source note. Official assessment and current terms still apply.

Verified Ladakh records: Passport size photograph; Aadhaar Card; BPL ration card; Account number with IFSC code; Widow certificate.
These are records listed for the reviewed regional implementation; they are not a nationwide checklist.
