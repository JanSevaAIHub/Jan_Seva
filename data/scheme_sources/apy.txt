State: National

Contributory retirement pension scheme with pension options linked to contributions.

Eligibility: The August 2024 Finance Ministry parliamentary answer describes Indian citizens aged 18 to 40 with a savings bank or post-office account. Income-tax payers are not eligible to join from October 1, 2022. This is a national scheme, not a Punjab-only pension.

Verified documents / enrolment records: Complete document list not verified from this source.

Evidence and limitations: The parliamentary answer describes minimum monthly pension choices of Rs. 1,000, 2,000, 3,000, 4,000 or 5,000, depending on joining age and the chosen pension. Contributions are required. This historical official source does not verify a complete document list or subsequent rule changes; applicants should check current official terms.
