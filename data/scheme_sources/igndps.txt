Scheme: Indira Gandhi National Disability Pension Scheme (IGNDPS)
State: Ladakh
Department: Ministry of Rural Development; Social Welfare and Tribal Welfare Department Ladakh
Scope: Central

Evidence type: reviewed paraphrase, not a verbatim quotation.

Pension support for people with severe disabilities.

Eligibility: The Ladakh government lists applicants aged at least 18, with disability of at least 80 percent, from a below-poverty-line household.

Coverage is limited to the Ladakh implementation reviewed here; other states and their document requirements need separate review.

This is a partial reviewed source note. Official assessment and current terms still apply.

Verified Ladakh records: Passport size photograph; Aadhaar Card; BPL ration card; Account number with IFSC code; UDID certificate with disability of at least 80 percent.
These are records listed for the reviewed regional implementation; they are not a nationwide checklist.
